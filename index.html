How this works: this page loads FFmpeg, compiled to WebAssembly, and runs it in your browser tab. Your video file is read locally and is never sent to any server. The page may refresh itself once on your first visit — that's a background step that lets your browser run FFmpeg safely, and it only happens once. The first conversion also loads a one-time ~25 MB engine file from a public CDN; after that it's cached by your browser.
